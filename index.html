01 / About Introduction

Design as a way of questioning the world around us.

I'm Zahra Aboul-Magd, an Egyptian-American multidisciplinary designer located in New York City.

My creative practice moves between clothing, objects, spatial exploration, and experimentation. This portfolio brings together selected projects across different design disciplines.

Education Parsons School of Design Location New York City, NY Practice Multidisciplinary Design
